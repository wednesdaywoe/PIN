# Attributes

Generated by `Tools/SdbDocs` from a build-1962 `clientdb.sd2` (db patch prod-1962, built 2016-05-05).

Items do not store their stats. They store rows in `dbitems::AttributeRange` keyed by an
attribute id, and this is what those ids mean. Two names matter and they are not the same: the
internal name is what a Red 5 designer typed and often says what the number was *for*, while the
display name is what the client puts on the item card. `Crater Damage` displays as `Base Damage`,
and several unrelated internal attributes share that one display name.

`Inverse` means lower is better, so the client renders an increase as a downward stat.
`Items` counts how many items carry the attribute; a zero means the attribute shipped but nothing
uses it, which is the usual shape of a cut feature.
