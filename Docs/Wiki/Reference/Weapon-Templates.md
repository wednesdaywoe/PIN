## Handling

Spread is the cone; rise and slide are the reticle climbing and drifting. All of it is computed
client-side from these fields, which is why retuning weapon feel would be a client edit and is
ruled out by [Restoration](../../Restoration.md).

`Jump spread` and `Run spread` are flat additions to the minimum cone while airborne or moving.
They are the fields that decide whether a weapon punishes a jetpack frame.
