DEPLOY-VEH-7 + DMG-LOOP-3[!] weapons do not splash — DATA-21Splash — find the ability first
It has to be an ability — no weapon splashes Confirmed 2026-08-16, from a Grenade Launcher fired between two touching enemies that killed neither. A weapon shot traces one ray and damages exactly what it struck; the grenade hit the ground, and the ground has no health. The blast radius is in the shipped ammunition data on 612 of 1264 rows and PIN never reads it. That is a real finding and it is now recorded as DATA-21 — but it is not DEPLOY-VEH-7, which needs the ability path, where splash actually is implemented.
Do the discovery step DMG-LOOP-3 has sat unrun purely for want of knowing which ability splashes. Find it here and you get both entries. Spawn two targets close together, fire each ability at the gap in turn, and look for one ExecutionId that produced two damage lines. That one execution damaging two entities is the splash. No weapon will ever produce it, however large the explosion looks on screen.
