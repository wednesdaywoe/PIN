The check on M7. The milestone is that a thumper is a fight: waves spawn while it drills, the machine itself can be destroyed, and the payout depends on how the defence went. Everything Thump Placement established still holds underneath — this stream only adds the part where something shoots back.

Closed 2026-08-15, 6 of 6, in one sitting on the morning the code landed — and it closed M7, the last milestone in the plan. The sitting ran three cycles where the doc planned two, because the first defended attempt was lost: the deliberate 20-second observation window THUMPER-DEF-3 asked for cost two-thirds of the pool, and wave 4's three sappers crossed the ring in ~2 seconds (they run 11 m/s) and erased the remaining 1300 health in four — destroyed at 91%, twenty-six seconds from the finish. The re-run was defended tightly, went 14 kills for 14 spawns, and paid 25 crystite at completion 1.00, defence 0.63 (1060/4000 health). The tester's wallet read 43, and the log's books balance it to the unit: 25 from the machine plus 18 across both cycles' kill drops. THUMPER-DEF-5 never had to be run deliberately — the losing path passed twice on its own, once on the zone's NPC-owned debug thumper before the tester was even in position (its own waves killed it, unattended, no crash), and once on the lost first cycle.

The reading that outlived the sitting: even a near-perfect solo defence pays ~63%, because claws land in the seconds between a sapper spawning and dying, four waves over. Full pay is effectively unreachable alone under current numbers. Options recorded, decision deferred (tester, 2026-08-15): slower claws, a bigger pool, or a softer payout curve. Two more tuning observations from the tester's seat: Aranha melee reach reads longer than it should, and the grenade launcher's AoE is too small for how spread the Aranha stay — they don't clump. None of it blocks the milestone; it's the first balance data the encounter has ever produced.

What was built, in one paragraph. During THUMPING, four waves of Melded Aranha (528) stand up on a 20m ring around the machine, at 15%, 40%, 65% and 90% progress — 45s, 120s, 195s and 270s into the 5-minute drill. Each wave splits into sappers, which march on the machine and ignore return fire, and escorts, which fight whoever they perceive and only chew the machine when nobody is around: 2+0, then 2+1, 3+1, 3+2, thirteen in total. The thumper now has a physics body and retail's own health pool (4000–5000 per the calldown def). Destroyed means failure: DESTROYED state, death ability, a completion event with the destroyed flag, no payout, wreck removed after 6 seconds. A survived defence pays completion x defence, where defence runs linearly from 0.5 at zero health to 1.0 untouched. Wave deaths route back to the encounter through the M2 death event; kill rewards pay per kill exactly as in Kill Rewards.

Two things a tester will notice that are physics, not bugs. The machine now blocks shots — your own rounds stop on it (friendly, so they do no damage), and a monster on the far side is in cover. And the waves spawn at the thumper's own height, so a thumper on a slope can stand its wave in the hillside; that is the standing bet every offset spawn makes and the reason deposits live on walked ground.

No new creature types: every wave member is 528, one of the two models ever seen rendering, so this stream triggers no CLIENT-3 facing check. That is deliberate. The first stream that puts a new monster id in a wave inherits the check.

The last one is THUMPER-DEF-3's diagnostic: the thumper's collision shape comes from the beacon's PosefileId asset (189562 or 250491), and if the deployment can't load it the physics engine substitutes a 0.9m sphere at the base. NPCs aim at 0.7m on structures for exactly that case, so the fight works either way, but the line says which body the machine actually has.

A defended run is still the full 7.5-minute cycle. Plan the sitting around two full cycles: one defended (THUMPER-DEF-1–THUMPER-DEF-4, THUMPER-DEF-6 all read from it), one abandoned (THUMPER-DEF-5).

THUMPER-DEF-1[x]A defended thumper pays, scaled by its health

The milestone's exit condition.

Passed 2026-08-15, on the second cycle. The first was destroyed at 91% (see THUMPER-DEF-5). The re-run: all four waves killed on sight, 14 for 14, collect at COMPLETED, and the payout line read completion 1.00, defence 0.63 (1060/4000 health, 14 attacker(s) down): 25 total at distance fraction 0.56 of deposit 1. The tester's on-screen 43 crystite is the session's whole ledger — 25 from the machine, 18 from kills — matched to the unit. The defence factor is honest and the number every future tuning pass argues with.

Pass: the thumper survives, pays, and the log states completion, defence, health and kills in one line. Fail, no waves ever spawn: check grep -a "wave" — if the spawn lines are absent the encounter never reached its progress thresholds (early collection skips remaining waves by design); if the lines are there but nothing stood up, that is the spawn path and N-stream territory.

THUMPER-DEF-3[x]Sappers hurt the machine

The entry that proves the collision body, which is the piece no offline test can touch.

Passed 2026-08-15, and the observation window it asked for is what lost the first cycle. The collision asset loaded — no fallback shape line for the thumper's body; the fallback lines in the log are all assetId 0, an unrelated pre-existing case — and the damage stream was immediate: 49 per claw, roughly one hit per second per sapper, so two sappers are ~100/s and three ~150/s against a 4000-point pool. The requested ~20 seconds of watching cost ~2000 health on its own. Health-bar movement on screen matched the log. The DPS reading is the sitting's main product: unopposed, one sapper kills the machine in 80 seconds, wave 4 alone in under 10.

Pass: server log shows damage landing and the client shows the same wound. Fail, no damage lines at all while sappers visibly claw the machine: their rounds are missing the body — the fallback-shape grep plus the NPC hold-fire lines (holding fire on ... OutOfRange / NoLineOfSight) say which half broke. Record the damage rate either way: nothing offline can size Aranha claws against a 4000-point pool, and this number is what tunes the wave schedule next.

THUMPER-DEF-4[x]Sappers ignore you, escorts don't

Passed 2026-08-15, tester-reported — the one entry only eyes can close. Sappers ignored being shot mid-walk, and kept ignoring the tester even when Charged clean through a group of them, which is a stronger provocation than the entry asked for. Escorts hunted the tester like any NPC-Combat monster. Two observations came out of the fighting itself, both deferred tuning rather than defects: the grenade launcher's small AoE never caught more than one Aranha because they don't group tightly enough, and Aranha melee reach reads longer than it should (DATA-10 territory — the 5m claw plus the 4m standoff means contact starts at range).

Pass: the two roles are visibly different on screen. This is the invented half of the design — the split is what makes defending a decision instead of a shooting gallery — so note anything that reads wrong, not just anything broken.

THUMPER-DEF-5[x]Losing the thumper costs the load

The failure path, on its own cycle.

Passed 2026-08-15 twice, and never by running the entry as written. First, unattended: the zone's NPC-owned debug thumper spawned its own wave at 09:02:23 — before the tester was in position — and its two sappers killed it by 09:03:16. destroyed ... at 32% with 0 attacker(s) down, survivors released, death ability ran, no crash, no payout. That is RESOURCE-PAYOUT-5's unattended-timer role inherited by M7: the debug thumper now loses its cycle every session, because nobody defends it — a changed zone behaviour to expect in future logs, not a defect. Second, live: the tester's first cycle died at 91% with 9 attackers down (see THUMPER-DEF-3 for why), five survivors released, no payout anywhere in the log. The client-side presentation of the destroyed state was not separately reported — the tester's attention was on the fight — so the wreck's on-screen reading rides along unconfirmed; everything server-side is exact.

Pass: the machine dies, the client shows a failed run, nothing pays, the swarm disperses. Fail, the cycle completes with the machine barely scratched: that is not a defect in the path, it is the DPS reading from THUMPER-DEF-3 saying the waves are undersized — record the final health and file it against the wave schedule rather than the code.
