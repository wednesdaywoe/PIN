Last run knockback worked on open ground (CONE-2 passed), and melee worked. Against things it went wrong: one Fiend was thrown inside an object, and one thrown at a steep wall jumped to the top of it.

Two causes. The flight never checked for anything in its path, so it could pass into objects. And it looked for the ground from 30 m above the monster, so beside a wall the first surface it found was the wall's top. Now each step of the flight checks for a wall at knee and chest height and stops half a metre short of it, then the monster drops straight down. The ground is looked for from just above its feet.

Some objects have no collision on the server at all, a known gap. A monster thrown at one of those will still pass into it, because the server can't see it. Step 3 tells the two apart.

CONE-4[x]A monster thrown at a wall or object stops against it
grep -aE "ActivateAbility 31366|knocked back|probe" ~/Games/PIN/logs/GameServer.log | tail -20
  • Pass: the Fiend stops against the wall or object and drops to the ground at its foot. It doesn't end up on top or inside.
  • Still inside an object, and probe says it hit nothing or something far behind it: that object has no collision on the server. That's the known gap, not this fix.
  • Still inside, and probe says it hit the object: the wall check is missing it. Record roughly how tall the object is; the checks are at 0.5 m and 1.2 m up.

Passed 2026-10-03. Fiends stopped against walls and rocks; none ended up inside geometry or on a wall top. Against some surfaces one sank into the ground for a moment and popped back up: carried on as CONE-5.

CONE-5[x]A landing monster doesn't sink and pop back up

Two possible causes. Your client may keep a falling monster falling past the last position it was sent, until the monster walks again; the landing position is now sent three times so the client sees it stop. Or the server may disagree with itself about the ground: landing looks from 1 m above the monster, walking from 30 m above, so under a leaning rock walking would lift it onto the rock. Each landing now logs both heights.

grep -aE "knocked back|landed at" ~/Games/PIN/logs/GameServer.log | tail -20
  • Pass: no sinking.
  • Still sinks: the landed at lines decide which cause. If the Z in the position and the walking would stand it at Z figure differ, the server disagrees with itself; if they match, it's the client's smoothing.

Passed 2026-10-03. No sinking. Both landings logged Z 401.14 against walking's 401.14, so the cause was the client carrying the fall on, fixed by repeating the landing pose.
