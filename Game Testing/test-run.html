Last run finished leaps and slams: Meteor Strike and Crater both work and can be repeated, and nothing gets stuck after landing.

This run has two parts. Do them in order; the ULT entries end with you dying, which is fine before the rest.

Part 1, the ultimate meter (ULT-1 to ULT-4). Until now it was permanently full, so ultimates could be fired any time. Now:

  • It starts empty each session and survives death.
  • It fills while you're in combat (you hit something, or got hit, in the last 10 seconds): about 2 minutes from empty to full.
  • Dealing damage adds a bonus: a whole monster's worth of health dealt adds 10% of the meter, at most 5% from one hit.
  • The ultimate module's Charge Speed (1 to 1.2) multiplies both.
  • An ultimate needs a full meter and empties it.

The rates are a first guess and easy to change. supercharge on its own shows the meter; supercharge 100 fills it.

Part 2, four abilities that did nothing until now (WAIT-1 to WAIT-3, PULL-1). Many abilities have a step that says "wait this long, then do this once". The server skipped that step, so whatever hung off it never happened. It now runs. These four depend on it:

  • Fuel Air Bomb (Firecat ultimate): the throw itself.
  • Assassinate (Raptor): the strike's damage.
  • Gravity Pull (Mammoth): its damage. The pull that drags enemies to you is also new: the server now throws each monster in an arc that lands 2.5 to 5 m in front of you.
  • Fungal Bloom (Recluse ultimate): the throw itself.

All four are equipped with equipitem onto whatever frame you wear. If one does nothing at all when pressed, switch to its own frame in the garage, equip it there, and try again; note which.
