About 350 abilities pick their targets as "everyone in a cone in front of you": melee strikes, Healing Wave, Thermal Wave, monster sweeps, and the use key's reach check. The server step that does this was empty, so every one of them found nobody. This commit builds it from the client's own version. The shape is a cone along your aim, starting at shoulder height. Some abilities use a tube that widens instead.

The test ability is Healing Wave, module 79018. It fits any frame. It fires a 15 m cone, 45° wide (22.5° either side of your aim). Friendlies inside are healed 300; enemies inside take 300 and are knocked back. Its recharge is 30 seconds.
