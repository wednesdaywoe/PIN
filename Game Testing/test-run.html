The current test run. This page is rewritten for every run; earlier runs are in git history. Setup, admin commands and the monster type id table: Session Setup.

Last run finished throw-then-trigger: a press while the fast Poison Ball flies sets it off, one ball per cooldown (DET-5).

This run is abilities that launch you: Meteor Strike's jump and dive, Crater's jump and slam. The server step that does the launching was empty, and your client only launches you itself for a few abilities. For the rest it waits for the server's instruction, so these abilities probably did nothing, or only played their animation.

The launch direction follows the client's own formula, read from the game's executable. One part is a guess: for a launch that isn't "along your current movement", the server uses where you're aiming. Meteor Strike is the test of that guess.

Both abilities belong to the Assault frame. Try equipping them on whatever frame you're wearing first; if the key does nothing, switch to an Assault frame (such as the Firecat) at the Battleframe Station and equip again.

IMP-1[x]Meteor Strike jumps you up, then dives you where you aim

From the game data: pressing it pushes you up for 1 second, then launches you at 45 m/s. On landing, enemies near you are damaged and knocked back (that part uses the knockback already tested with the cone attacks).

grep -aE "ApplyImpulseCommand|ForcePushCommand|Ability 39243" ~/Games/PIN/logs/GameServer.log | tail -30
  • Pass: you rise, then dive to roughly where you aimed. The log has ApplyImpulseCommand 1618797 launches (the rise) and about a second later ApplyImpulseCommand 1634936 launches (the dive, about 45 in length, pointing down along your aim). The Fiends are thrown back on landing.
  • The dive goes flat or the wrong way: the aim guess is wrong. Note the direction you went relative to where you aimed.
  • The launches lines are there but you don't move: the client ignored the push message. Note what you saw instead.
  • No launches lines at all: the ability never reached the server's launch step. Note whether the animation played.

Passed 2026-10-03. Meteor Strike rises and dives along the aim; the log shows the rise (1618797, up 5 m/s plus running speed), the dive (1634936, 45 m/s pointing down along the aim), and the shove on landing. The aim guess holds.

IMP-2[!]Crater jumps you up, then slams you straight down

From the game data: pressing it pushes you up, keeping your running speed. 1.3 seconds later you're driven straight down at 30 m/s, and on landing nearby enemies are damaged and thrown.

grep -aE "ApplyImpulseCommand|ForcePushCommand|Ability 212 " ~/Games/PIN/logs/GameServer.log | tail -30
  • Pass: you rise, then drop hard straight down, and running carries you forward on the rise. The log has ApplyImpulseCommand 1154093 launches, then about 1.3 s later ApplyImpulseCommand 818397 launches ... <0, 0, -30>. Fiends near the landing are thrown back.
  • You rise but are never slammed down: the second push didn't reach the client. Note whether a 818397 line is in the log.

Failed 2026-10-03: works once, then never again. The first Crater rose, slammed down and shoved (log 21:29:50). Every later press failed at the first step. Cause: Crater gives you a lockout effect (8322) only if your module has the Crater Dome attribute, and lasts until you change battleframe. Both of those checks were unbuilt and always said yes, so every Crater locked itself forever. Both are now built; see IMP-4.

A forced move tells the client to take over your movement for a moment. Earlier work on Charge showed a forced move that never ends can lock the camera or controls.

  • Pass: everything works straight after landing.
  • Stuck camera, no movement, or sliding: say which ability and what's stuck. Relogging clears it.
IMP-4[x]Crater can be used again after its cooldown

Two server checks are now real. "Does your module have this attribute?" reads your equipped items, the way the client does: a value of zero counts as no. "Until you change battleframe" now ends an effect when you switch to a different frame. Your Crater (86369) has no dome attribute, so it should never take the lockout. About 40 other equippable abilities use the attribute check, so a different ability that suddenly refuses to fire is worth reporting.
