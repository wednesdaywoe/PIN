Last run (commit 8a7c040) the cone attacks worked. Healing Wave healed a friendly in front of you and not one behind (CONE-1 passed), hit a Fiend in front for 300, and the use key still worked (CONE-3 passed). The Fiend wasn't knocked back.

The push step was a stand-in: it only ever pushed players, straight up, and skipped every monster. Monsters are moved only by the server, so this commit gives them a flight: a pushed monster is thrown along an arc away from you, can't walk or shoot until it lands, and its position is sent out every 50 ms as it flies. The client's data says how hard and how high; how far that carries is PIN's choice. Healing Wave's push should carry a Fiend about 6 m in two thirds of a second.
