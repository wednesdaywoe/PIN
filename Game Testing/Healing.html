Tests commit 72cc765. Until it, the ability step that heals — HealDamage, in 365 abilities — was never created, so every heal in the game did nothing on the server. The client only shows a heal as combat text; health is the server's to change.

Every entry uses ability 31217 (SDBQuery ability 31217). It finds everyone within 10 m of you, keeps the friendly ones other than you, and heals each by 300. It also puts a cooldown on itself, which the server enforces: 60 seconds in the data, scaled by a bonus value the server reads as 0.833, so 50 seconds in practice. Whether the client agrees on 50 is unchecked, since the admin path isn't predicted.

A friendly character can't be shot, so sethealth was added to wound one: sethealth 100 sets the current target's health (yours with no target), and sethealth on its own reports it.

HEAL-1[x]A wounded friendly NPC is healed by 300

The main check.

grep -aE "HealDamageCommand|31217 (inflicts|refused|failed)|Chain 1356(16|08) Command" ~/Games/PIN/logs/GameServer.log | tail -20
  • Pass: the log has HealDamageCommand 135607 healed CharacterEntity (...) for 300 of 300, then HealDamageCommand 1365339 ... for 40 of 40 repeating for 5 seconds, and step 5 reads 1200 (100 + 300 + 20 ticks of 40), or the max if lower. The ability also puts effect 445 on whoever it heals, a 5-second heal over time; this page first expected 400 because it missed that.
  • Fail, no healed line and the chain log stops at TargetFriendlies: nobody friendly was found within 10 m. Stand closer and repeat after the 60-second cooldown. If it still stops there, the NPC isn't being read as friendly; run hostility on it and record the reply.
  • Fail, a healed line but the health didn't move: the heal reached the server's health and not the NPC's views. Record both.

Passed 2026-10-02. NPC 290 spawned with max health 1224, set to 100. ability 31217 at 22:56:10 logged healed ... for 300 of 300, effect 445 ran 22:56:10–22:56:15 with 20 ticks of for 40 of 40, and sethealth read 1200 of 1224.
